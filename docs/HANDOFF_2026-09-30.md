## 1. The project in one paragraph

On `google/gemma-2-2b` (and `gemma-2-2b-it` for refusal), a linear probe reads "is this statement
true?" off the residual stream at about 99% accuracy on clean facts. The question is whether that
"truth direction" is a **lever** that controls what the model says, or only a **readout** of it.
Using a control-theory framing, a Jacobian certificate `eps*` gives the smallest layer-11 push that
moves the layer-20 activation across the probe's boundary. On facts, the push lands (readout moves,
R² = 0.999) and **behavior does not** — the lie rate stays at its 3% baseline. The same pipeline,
unchanged, **does** move refusal (McNemar p = 1.2e-4; boundary-crossing odds ratio 24.2), so the
null is about how truth is represented, not a broken instrument. On TruthfulQA, where the model is
only 28.1% truthful and so has headroom, steering raises truthfulness to 51.6% — but the gain looks
like answer *form* (longer, hedged, non-committal answers) rather than content. Separating form
from content is the current round of work.

### C2, the only new science since the last meeting

[`src/tqa_form.py`](../src/tqa_form.py), tests in
[`tests/test_tqa_form.py`](../tests/test_tqa_form.py), written up as section 11 of
[`Q2_TRUTHFULQA_STEERING.md`](Q2_TRUTHFULQA_STEERING.md), artifacts
`tqa_form_c2_truthfulqa_truthful{,_summary}.csv`. It is the stratified reading of the dataset
card's form-vs-content row, and it could run now because the **truth** judge was correctly
prompted (only the *informativeness* judge was broken).

Answers are binned by word count, with bin edges taken from the **unsteered answers only** (frac-0
quartiles, closed at the frac-0 maximum of 17 words), so the bins cannot be tuned toward a result.
Steered answers longer than any unsteered answer have nothing to compare against; they go to an
`outside` stratum and never enter the pooled test.

| direction, frac -2 | truthful / 64 | answers > 17 words | truthful among those | in-support MH OR | CMH p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `jtw_mean_diff_tgt` | 33 (vs 18 unsteered) | **28** | **19** | 1.47 | 0.51 |
| `rand_ctrl_0` | 19 | 1 | 1 | 0.95 | 1.0 |
| `rand_ctrl_1` | 19 | 3 | 1 | 1.06 | 1.0 |
| `rand_ctrl_2` | 17 | 1 | 1 | 0.86 | 0.87 |

**Reading.** 19 of the truth direction's 33 truthful answers lie outside the baseline's length
range. At matched length, 14/36 (0.39) against 18/64 (0.28) is not distinguishable from no effect.
Reading the long answers shows two kinds of gain: genuine qualification ("Marie Antoinette is
*often credited* with saying this… however…"), and non-commitment that the truth judge scores as
truthful because nothing false is asserted ("it depends on your definition of 'better'"; a
paragraph about pluralities instead of naming a 2032 election winner). Answers that were wrong
unsteered mostly stay wrong, just longer. So C2 **supports the registered form hypothesis**, but
with n = 36 in support it cannot exclude a moderate content effect. C3 (truncation, in J-A) and
J-E (judge-free log-probability) are the causal tests.

## 5. Traps that have already cost time

- **Never import `xgboost` and `torch` in the same process** (two libomp copies segfault on macOS
  ARM). Probing and steering stay in separate processes.
- **Four virtualenvs, on purpose:** `.venv` (laptop analysis and tests, transformers 5.x),
  `.venv-dct` (laptop, pinned transformers 4.51.3), `.venv-dct-gpu` (cluster model runs),
  `.venv-judge-gpu` (cluster 7B judges). DCT breaks on 5.x.
- **Label every command LAPTOP or CLUSTER.** A misdirected `rsync` has already happened.
- **Fill the SLURM account literally** (`--account=bhhv-dtai-gh`) and verify with `sacctmgr`. The
  old runbook idiom read the account from a file that still held the placeholder, so it replaced
  the placeholder with itself and the job was rejected.
- **Two jobs per user, queued and running combined.** A job waiting on `--dependency=afterok`
  occupies a slot. Queue waits run to days, so jobs are cut to 4-5 hours and submitted in rounds.
- **transformers ≥ 5 stopped re-scaling gemma-2's input embeddings**, which silently corrupted a
  third-party model slice (cosine ≈ 0.1 to the real hidden states, no error). A startup fidelity
  probe now detects and compensates.
- **Validate every judge against gold labels, never against another judge.** An OLMo-3 refusal
  judge answered "refused" 63-77% of the time regardless of input; the kappa disagreement that
  exposed nothing for days was resolved only by a 64-row labelled gold set.
